03 / About

Design-minded.
Story-driven.

I turn complex ideas into clear, compelling visuals — with a designer’s eye and a storyteller’s instinct.

I’m a motion designer and animator with 3+ years of experience in 2D and 3D animation, motion graphics and video editing. I’ve produced high-impact marketing campaigns, explainer videos and brand content for the energy, SaaS, broadcast media and e-learning sectors — and I’m known for juggling multiple projects under tight deadlines without losing creative or brand quality.

My client work spans Kenya, Rwanda and Uganda, including Kajenga Private Bank, Kenya Premier Bank, I&M Bank and PSF/KEPSA. I’m also building Aurora Creative Studio, a practice exploring design, animation, branding and digital content.
